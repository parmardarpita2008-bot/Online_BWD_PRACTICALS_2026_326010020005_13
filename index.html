About Marwadi University

A State Private University located in Rajkot, offering world-class education.

Marwadi University (MU) is known for its state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and strong industry linkages. We provide a vibrant learning ecosystem across various streams like Engineering, Computer Applications, Business Management, Science, and Pharmacy.
